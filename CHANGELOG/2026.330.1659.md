## Change classification

**No breaking change in this repo.** The builder bakes pixi 0.84.2 and composes successfully (pasted
above), so every box here still resolves. The consumer-visible constraint is pixi's own: a candy lock
resolved for a bare `linux-64` must declare `[system-requirements] libc = 2.39` (the platform the
builder appends), and a lock resolved for that platform installs `--frozen` on 0.84.2 as it did on
0.70.2. Reverting the pin restores the older binary.
