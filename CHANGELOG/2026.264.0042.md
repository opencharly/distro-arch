## Wave / landing order (cross-repo cutover)

This is the PRODUCER of a three-PR wave. The other two PRs cannot exist before
this one merges (the marketplace pin must reference the CalVer tag this merge
mints, and the aggregator deletion must follow that pin so the generation
closure never loses the skills). The wave lands in this fixed order, with each
PR titled exactly as named:

1. **this PR — `opencharly/distro-arch`** `feat(distros): own the arch-family skills in distro-arch`
   (adds the entities; merge mints `v<CalVer>`).
2. **`opencharly/marketplace`** `chore(refs): pin distro-arch:v<CalVer> + regenerate`
   — adds `@github.com/opencharly/distro-arch:v<CalVer>` to
   `candy/charly-marketplace/charly.yml` and regenerates; its drift gate proves
   the corpus is byte-identical.
3. **`opencharly/layer-charly-distros`** `refactor(distros): drop the arch-family skills (moved to distro-arch)`
   — deletes the seven now-duplicated entities.

Between this merge and step 3, the seven entities exist in both repos — the
unavoidable transient of a repo-split cutover across three independently-landing
repos (they cannot share one PR). It is bounded to this wave: step 2 and step 3
are opened and landed immediately, and no further skill moves begin until step 3
is merged. After step 3, `git grep` for each moved entity returns only the
aggregator's CHANGELOG history.
